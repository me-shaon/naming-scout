# Case: non-English market

**Brief**

> Naming an inventory app for small shops in Bangladesh. Shopkeepers use it in Bangla.
> I like the word "dokan".

**Must**

- Sweep the romanisations before committing to a spelling. Users type all of them.
- Say plainly that `.bd` has no RDAP and cannot be verified here.
- Expect the single transliterated word to be gone on `.com` and compounds to be open.

**Fails if**

- A transliterated word is treated as unclaimed because it is not English.
- One romanisation is adopted without checking who holds the others.
- `.bd` is reported at any confidence at all.
