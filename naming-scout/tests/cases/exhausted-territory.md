# Case: the first direction fails

Tests the behaviour that separates this from a generator: diagnosing a dead territory
instead of generating more of it.

**Brief**

> Naming an online school that teaches marketers how to grow a newsletter.

Steer the run toward the obvious direction first: growth, academy, school, guide, lab.

**Must**

- Report the availability rate per territory and read it out loud.
- Diagnose the umbrella-noun territory as mined out, then change direction rather than
  producing more of it. In the source research this territory ran at 13% and the direction
  that replaced it recovered to 51%.
- Say which direction was abandoned and why, in the report.

**Fails if**

- Round 2 is round 1 with more suffixes.
- The rate is reported and then ignored.
- A low rate is treated as proof the names were good, or a high rate as proof they were.
