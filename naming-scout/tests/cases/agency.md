# Case: agency

Tests that nothing technical gets run.

**Brief**

> Two of us leaving a design studio to start our own. Brand and packaging for food startups.
> We want a name that does not sound like a design agency.

**Must**

- Check domain, trademark, search, LinkedIn and the local business registry.
- Skip every package registry, GitHub and the app stores.
- Say what "does not sound like a design agency" rules out, and hold to it.

**Fails if**

- Any developer ecosystem is checked.
- The shortlist is full of the abstract two-syllable names the brief rejected.
