# Discovery

Transliteration of non-Latin scripts (Bangla, etc.) produces large variant sets; single transliterated words are nearly all registered on .com, only compound variants have availability. Example: 'dokan' family has 4 available, 5 for_sale, 12 registered across 21 variants.
