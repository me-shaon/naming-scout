# Discovery

Agent skill repos in the wild follow mixed licensing practices: anthropics/skills and vercel-labs/agent-skills ship without LICENSE files, while obra/superpowers and vercel-labs/skills use MIT. First-party Anthropic and Vercel repos can rely on implicit corporate licensing; individual repos should include explicit LICENSE to permit cloning and redistribution that `npx skills add` requires.
